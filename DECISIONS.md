# Design Decisions – BhaktiPath

## 1. Why I chose this approach

I chose to build the Premium Home Page around BhaktiPath, a spiritual and cultural website focused on devotional content. The goal was to create a simple, welcoming and visually engaging experience where users can explore deities, festivals, bhajans, sacred texts and temples.

I used a hero section with a clear value proposition and a "Start Your Journey" call-to-action. I also added interactive content such as the Daily Shlok section and expandable content cards to make the experience more engaging.

## 2. Trade-offs made due to the time limit

Because of the limited development time, I focused on delivering a complete and functional experience rather than adding unnecessary features.

I prioritized:
- A clear hero section and CTA
- Responsive layout
- Interactive content
- Simple animations and hover effects
- Multiple useful content sections
- A clean and consistent visual style

I avoided complex backend functionality, authentication and unnecessary animations so that the website could remain stable and usable within the available time.

## 3. AI tools and verification

AI tools were used to assist with development and improve the structure and presentation of the website.

I reviewed and tested the generated/assisted code manually after using AI. I verified the navigation, interactive elements, styling, page structure and deployment to ensure that the final website worked correctly.
